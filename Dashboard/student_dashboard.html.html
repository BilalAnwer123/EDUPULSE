Key takeaways. Attendance and quiz performance are the strongest warning signals: students below 60% attendance fail about 52% of the time, versus about 6% at 90% or above. Students averaging under 4 on quizzes fail about 42% of the time. Failing students trail passing students on every measure, with the widest gaps in quizzes and mid marks.
